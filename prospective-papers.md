## Suggested coverage

To present a coherent but varied session, target commitments across these areas:

1. Dynamic or stochastic vehicle routing and logistics.
2. Multi-objective traffic control or transport network design.
3. Public transport, shared mobility, or demand-responsive transport.
4. Electric fleet planning, charging, or energy-aware routing.
5. Simulation-based optimization, digital twins, or infrastructure planning.
