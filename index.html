Proposed Special Session | IEEE CEC 2027

Evolutionary Computation for Sustainable Transportation and Logistics

25–28 July 2027 | Edinburgh, Scotland, UK

This proposed special session focuses on the development and application of evolutionary computation methods for optimisation problems in transportation and logistics. It aims to bring together researchers working on evolutionary algorithms, intelligent transportation systems, logistics and operations research.

View Topics of Interest

Overview and Scope

Transportation and logistics involve complex optimisation problems characterised by dynamic operating conditions, uncertain demand, large search spaces and multiple, often conflicting objectives. These characteristics present important challenges for the design and application of evolutionary computation methods.

This special session aims to bring together researchers working on evolutionary algorithms and transportation-related optimisation. It welcomes methodological contributions, hybrid optimisation approaches and application-oriented studies addressing realistic problems in transportation, mobility and logistics.

Particular interest is given to methods that address uncertainty, scalability, computational efficiency, operational constraints and multi-objective decision-making.

Topics of Interest

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following:

  • Evolutionary algorithms for vehicle routing and scheduling
  • Dynamic and stochastic transportation optimisation
  • Traffic signal control and transportation network optimisation
  • Public transport, shared mobility and demand-responsive transportation
  • Sustainable mobility and emission-aware optimisation
  • Electric vehicle routing, charging and fleet management
  • Multi-objective and many-objective optimisation in transportation
  • Transportation infrastructure planning, maintenance and resilience
  • Simulation-based and surrogate-assisted optimisation
  • Evolutionary computation for logistics and supply chain management
  • Hybrid evolutionary algorithms and matheuristics
  • Data-driven evolutionary optimisation and neuroevolution

Relevance to IEEE CEC 2027

The proposed session is closely aligned with the scope of IEEE CEC, particularly in evolutionary optimisation, constrained optimisation, multi-objective search, dynamic optimisation and real-world applications.

Transportation and logistics provide challenging problems for investigating the performance and practical applicability of evolutionary algorithms. The session encourages contributions that advance evolutionary computation methods, provide rigorous experimental evaluations, or demonstrate their applicability to complex transportation systems.

The session also aims to encourage exchanges between researchers in evolutionary computation, transportation engineering and operations research.
