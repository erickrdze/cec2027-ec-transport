Proposed Special Session | IEEE CEC 2027

Evolutionary Computation for Sustainable Transportation and Logistics

25–28 July 2027 | Edinburgh, Scotland, UK

This proposed special session focuses on the development and application of evolutionary computation methods for optimisation problems in transportation and logistics. It aims to bring together researchers working on evolutionary algorithms, intelligent transportation systems, logistics and operations research.

View Topics of Interest

Overview and Scope

Transportation and logistics involve complex optimisation problems characterised by dynamic operating environments, uncertain demand, large search spaces, operational constraints and multiple, often conflicting objectives.

This special session brings together research on evolutionary computation and transportation-related optimisation. It welcomes evolutionary and hybrid optimisation methods, as well as application-oriented studies addressing realistic problems in transportation, mobility and logistics.

The session welcomes methodological advances motivated by transportation problems and applied studies that contribute to the design, evaluation or adaptation of evolutionary algorithms. Particular attention is given to uncertainty, scalability, realistic operational constraints, and the balance between solution quality and computational efficiency. Comparative studies and reproducible experimental evaluations are also encouraged.

Topics of Interest

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following:

Transportation and Logistics Applications

  • Vehicle routing and schedulingDynamic and stochastic vehicle routing, fleet coordination, last-mile delivery, and transportation scheduling.
  • Traffic and network optimisationTraffic signal control, congestion management, network design, incident management, and adaptive traffic operations.
  • Public transport and shared mobilityDemand-responsive transport, multimodal coordination, ride-sharing, service planning, and mobility-on-demand.
  • Sustainable and electric mobilityElectric vehicle routing, charging infrastructure, energy-aware fleet management, and emission-aware optimisation.
  • Transportation infrastructure and resilienceRoadworks planning, infrastructure maintenance, network disruptions, robust planning, and recovery strategies.
  • Logistics and supply chainsFreight transport, urban logistics, distribution networks, and multi-echelon logistics optimisation.

Evolutionary Computation Methods and Challenges

  • Multi-objective and robust optimisationConflicting objectives involving travel time, cost, emissions, accessibility, safety, and service quality.
  • Dynamic and uncertain optimisationReal-time adaptation, time-dependent constraints, uncertain demand, and changing operating conditions.
  • Simulation-based and surrogate-assisted optimisationTraffic simulation, digital twins, computationally expensive evaluations, and surrogate modelling.
  • Hybrid and learning-assisted evolutionary methodsMemetic algorithms, matheuristics, hyper-heuristics, neuroevolution, and integration with machine learning.
  • Scalable and distributed evolutionary optimisationLarge-scale transportation networks, parallel optimisation, cooperative search, and multi-agent coordination.
  • Benchmarking and reproducible evaluationRealistic problem formulations, comparative studies, simulation environments, and reproducible experimental protocols.

Relevance to IEEE CEC 2027

The session is aligned with several core areas of IEEE CEC, including constrained, dynamic, stochastic, multi-objective, and large-scale evolutionary optimisation. Transportation and logistics provide challenging problems for evaluating these methods under realistic operational conditions, computational limitations, and uncertain environments.

By connecting methodological research with practical optimisation problems, the session aims to encourage contributions that advance evolutionary computation and provide rigorous evaluations of its applicability to transportation and logistics systems.
